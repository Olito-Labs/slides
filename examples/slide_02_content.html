Most teams have tools — few have capability

What Teams Have

Enterprise AI licenses
Copilot, ChatGPT Team, Claude Pro — paid for but underused across the organization.
Prompt libraries
Shared collections of templates that quickly become stale and fail to capture real workflows.
Training sessions
One-off workshops that generate enthusiasm but no lasting behavior change.

What Teams Need

Workflow integration
AI embedded in the actual work — not a separate step, but part of the existing process.
Skill building
Hands-on practice with real deliverables, guided by someone who builds every day.
Accountability structure
Regular checkpoints and measurable progress — not just inspiration, but transformation.
The gap isn't access. It's adoption.
